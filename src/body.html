What the import found

Every year-sheet in the workbook was read cell by cell. A reservation in that format is a run of same-coloured cells with a name in the first one, so the reader reconstructs each stay from the fill runs and merged ranges rather than from any stored start and end date. What follows is what came out, and it is the argument for replacing the grid.

Overlapping assignments

Two occupants booked onto the same berth at the same time. On a 410′ pier that may be perfectly legal — two vessels fit alongside each other. The grid cannot tell you which, because it records neither position nor length.
